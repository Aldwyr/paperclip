How bright can it go?

This ramp uses Sunflower (option 14), the unchanged white foreground, and the same borderless geometry. At 15px / 400 weight the text is “normal text,” so WCAG 2.2 AA requires at least 4.5:1 contrast. Sixty percent is the brightest practical stop here at 4.66:1; 62% falls below the requirement.
